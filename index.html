Events & Activities

Engagement

The AI Lab is committed to educating and equipping students and the broader community with AI knowledge and skills.

Workshop

Faculty Workshop on AI

A comprehensive workshop equipping Biola faculty across multiple departments with AI literacy and pedagogical strategies for integrating AI into their coursework.

Speaker Series

AI Lab Speaker Series

Hosted thought leaders, researchers, and practitioners to explore the theological and ethical dimensions of artificial intelligence.

Student Engagement

AI Club & Student Workshops

Engaged with over 100 students through interactive workshops and discussions on the future of work, Christianity and AI, and hands-on AI experiences.

Collaboration

AI Lab × Torrey Honors College

Cross-disciplinary collaboration bringing AI education into Torrey's great books tradition — asking what it means to be human in an age of AI.
